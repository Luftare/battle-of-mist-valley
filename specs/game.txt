It's an auto-battler.

It's a square play area with 8 building slots at each side.

Each building auto-spawns corresponding unit type, once every 15 sec.

The units are spawned next to the building, towards the enemy side.

Units start moving towards the enemy. They do some trivial path finding so that they don't walk at the trees and rocks but walk around them.

Once enemy comes at shoot distance, they start shooting at the enemy.

Units have hp and each hit reduces some hp.

HP bar is shown above each unit.

Units prioritize other enemy units, when no enemy unit is at shoot range, they shoot at buildings that are within range.

Units may have movement target at which they move and upon reaching it, they stop.

Tanks need to have their turret facing the enemy unit before they can shoot.

Place random buildings in each slot.

Destroyed units slowly sink in the ground and after 15 sec disappear.
